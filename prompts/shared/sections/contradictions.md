#### contradictions
Two or more facts that cannot both be true.
- Example: BR-2 "下單後 3 天內可取消" and BR-7 "7 天內都可以取消" → conflict "取消期限不一致：3 天 vs 7 天".
- `relatedIds`: at least two ids of the conflicting facts. Evidence is taken from those facts automatically.
- Facts that differ in a value are contradictions, NOT duplicates. Never merge them.
