#### domainEntities (id prefix ENT)
Business objects the system must record, which have attributes or states.
- Example: 訂單 with attributes 狀態, 金額.
- Placement: a noun with attributes or states goes here. A noun that is only a word to define goes in glossary. Do not record the same noun in both.
- `relationships`: links to other ENT ids stated in the documents, e.g. { targetId: "ENT-2", kind: "has-many" }.
