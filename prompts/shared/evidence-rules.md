### Evidence rules
- `file` is the path exactly as written in `<document path="...">`.
- `lineStart` and `lineEnd` are the numbers after `L` on the first and last cited line. Never cite the `L<n>: ` prefix itself.
- `quote` is copied character-for-character from the cited lines. Do not paraphrase, shorten with "...", fix typos or translate.
- Quote the whole sentence or line that supports the item, not just one word.
- One item may have several evidence entries when several places support it.
